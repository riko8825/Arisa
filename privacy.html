Privacy Notice

Last updated 10 May 2026. Written in plain language because legalese has no place in a nursery.

Arisa in WonderDolls (“we”, “us”) is a small atelier registered in Vilnius, Lithuania. We collect only the information we need to send you a doll, write you a letter, or answer a question. This notice explains how.

What we collect

  • Order & adoption details — your name, shipping address, email, telephone (optional) and order contents.
  • Payment information — handled directly by Stripe and (where chosen) your bank. We never see your full card number.
  • Letters & messages — what you write to us via the contact form, email, or social channels.
  • Newsletter — your email and name only, with your consent, and unsubscribe at any time.
  • Site analytics — anonymous aggregate visit data (no personal profiling, no advertising trackers).

Why we use it

To fulfil your order, ship your doll safely, answer your questions, send the occasional studio letter (only if you opt in), and meet our legal obligations as a small EU business.

Who we share it with

Only the people we need to: our payment processor (Stripe), our shipping carrier, our email host, and the Lithuanian tax authority where required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. Ever.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for ten years to comply with EU accounting law. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Letters are kept as long as we are in conversation, and then archived gently.

Your rights

You may, at any time, request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it (where legally possible), or withdraw any consent you have given. Write to arisainwonderdolls@gmail.com and we will respond within thirty days.

Cookies

We use a single, essential cookie to remember the contents of your basket. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. If we ever introduce optional analytics, you will be asked first.

Contact & complaints

If you would like to raise a concern, please write to us first; we take this seriously. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI).
