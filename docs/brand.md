# Brand — Arisa in WonderDolls

## Essence

A small atelier of one-of-one reborn dolls, made by hand by Arisa.
Quiet. Tender. Editorial. Personal.

**Not:** mass-produced toy store, "kawaii", "cute", high-energy retail.

## Tagline & one-liners

- **Tagline:** Unique reborn dolls for collectors and dreamers
- **Hero hook:** Quietly made, tenderly held.
- **About:** A doll is finished only when it begins to breathe a little on its own.
- **CTA leaning:** "Begin an adoption", "Visit the nursery", "Meet Arisa"
- **CTA NOT:** "Buy now", "Shop today", "Get yours"

### Typography

| Role | Font | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Cormorant Garamond | 400, italic 300 | H1, H2, hero, product names |
| Body | Inter | 300, 400, 500 | Paragraphs, nav, buttons |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono | 400 | Eyebrows, prices, meta, "ATELIER · LITHUANIA" |

**Italic swashes** — Cormorant italic 300 — reserved for **emotional words**: "tenderly", "souls", "in WonderDolls", "looking for a home".

## Photography direction

When real photos arrive, brief Arisa to shoot:
- **Soft natural light** — morning, north-facing window, no flash
- **Neutral backdrops** — linen, cream paper, raw wood
- **Macro details** — eyelashes, fingernails, mohair strands, paint texture
- **Hand-in-frame** — Arisa's hand cradling doll = brand story
- **No sterile catalog shots** — every photo should feel like a still life, not a product page
