Frequently asked

A few
gentle answers.

If your question is not here, write — it is no trouble.

How do I adopt a doll from the nursery?

Reserve the doll from her page; she will be held for thirty minutes while you complete payment. We accept Stripe (card & Apple Pay) or manual SEPA bank transfer with a 10% deposit.

How long does a bespoke commission take?

Between eight and twelve weeks from confirmation, depending on the studio queue. You will receive painting-stage photographs at three milestones along the way.

Are these dolls suitable for children?

Reborn dolls are collectors' pieces and best enjoyed by older children with adult guidance. They are not toys and are not suitable for under-fives.

Can I return a doll?

Yes — within 14 days of arrival, in original condition. Bespoke commissions are non-returnable, but lifetime gentle restoration is included.

Do you ship outside Thailand?

Yes, worldwide, with full insurance and tracking. Domestic Thai shipping is included; international shipping is calculated transparently at checkout.
