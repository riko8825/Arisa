Terms & Conditions

A short, plainly-written agreement between the atelier and the people who adopt from it.

These terms apply to every adoption made through arisainwonderdolls.com. By placing an order, you confirm that you have read and accept them.

1. The atelier

Arisa in WonderDolls is a sole-trader business operated by Arisa Vaitkevičiūtė, registered in Vilnius, Lithuania (LT). Contact: arisainwonderdolls@gmail.com.

2. Each doll is one-of-one

All reborn dolls are individually hand-made art objects. Subtle variations between the photographs and the final piece are part of the work. We provide as faithful a representation as possible, in natural light.

3. Reservations & payment

Reserving a doll holds her privately for thirty minutes while you complete checkout. Payment is taken via Stripe (card & Apple Pay) or, on request, via SEPA bank transfer with a non-refundable 10% deposit. The remainder is due before dispatch.

4. Shipping

EU shipping is included. Worldwide shipping is calculated at checkout. All parcels are fully insured and tracked. We dispatch within 3 working days of cleared payment, or — for commissions — at the agreed studio date.

5. Returns & aftercare

Nursery dolls may be returned within 14 days of arrival in original, unworn condition. Bespoke commissions are non-returnable. We offer lifetime gentle restoration on every doll we make — please write to us first before returning anything.

6. Suitability

Reborn dolls are collectors' pieces. They are not toys and are not suitable for children under five. Older children should enjoy them with adult guidance.

7. Intellectual property

All photographs, written content, and the brand name “Arisa in WonderDolls” remain the property of the atelier. You are warmly welcome to share photographs of your doll on personal social media with a credit.

8. Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by Lithuanian law, our liability is limited to the value of the doll purchased. This does not affect your statutory consumer rights under EU law.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Lithuania. Any disputes will be resolved in the courts of Vilnius, unless EU consumer protection rules entitle you to bring the matter elsewhere.

10. Changes

We may update these terms from time to time. The most current version will always live at this address, with the revision date at the top of this page.
