Liora

A sleeping newborn with a quiet, dreaming face. Painted in soft warm tones, with rooted ash-blonde mohair and a barely-there blush at the nose.

What arrives in the cradle box

Liora ships in a linen-lined wooden cradle box with a hand-written letter from Arisa, a numbered adoption certificate, a soft cotton outfit, a pacifier, a small heirloom blanket, and a printed care guide.

Shipping & insurance

We ship worldwide via fully insured express courier, packed and double-boxed. EU shipping is free; rest-of-world is calculated at checkout. Arrival typically within 3–7 working days from dispatch.

Returns & aftercare

You may return your reborn within 14 days of arrival in unworn, unwashed condition. Lifetime gentle-restoration is included for any doll that ever needs mending — please write to the studio first.

Care for your reborn

Keep her out of direct sunlight, away from ink and felt-tip, and gently dust her with a soft brush. The mohair can be combed lightly with a doll-safe wig brush. A full care leaflet ships with the cradle.

A note from the maker

“Liora was painted in February, in a week of unusually soft snow. I think a little of that quiet stayed in her cheeks.”
