From the atelier

A doll is finished only when it begins to breathe a little on its own.

Arisa works in slow, quiet rounds — a wash of warmth on the cheek, a single eyelash placed with tweezers, a vein traced so faintly you have to look twice. Nothing here is mass-produced. Nothing here is the same.

Read the atelier story
The making

Six to ten weeks,
held in patient hands.

Each reborn moves through the same gentle ritual — a slow apprenticeship between sculpt and skin. We never skip a layer; we never duplicate a face.

Choosing the sculpt

We work only with limited-edition vinyl kits from artist sculptors we love. Each kit is signed and numbered before it ever reaches a brush.

Skin in many layers

Twenty to thirty translucent washes of Genesis heat-set paint. Capillaries, blushes, milia, the faintest hint of newborn down.

Hair, eyes & lashes

Single strands of European mohair, micro-rooted by hand. German glass eyes set in beeswax. Lashes placed one at a time.

Weighting & assembly

Glass beads and soft fibre give each doll the warmth and weight of a sleeping child. The cloth body is hand-stitched in soft cotton.
